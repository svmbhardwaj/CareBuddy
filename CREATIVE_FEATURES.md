## 🫁 **Breathing Exercise Button**

### What It Does
Guides you through a **4-7-8 breathing technique** with visual feedback!

### Features
✅ **Guided Breathing** - Professional breathing cycle
✅ **Visual Circle** - Expanding/contracting circle guides your breathing
✅ **4 Phases**:
   - 4 seconds: Breathe In 🫁
   - 7 seconds: Hold ⏸️
   - 8 seconds: Breathe Out 💨
   - 2 seconds: Calm ✨

✅ **Beautiful Modal** - Centered, gradient-styled modal
✅ **Overlay** - Click outside to close
✅ **Success Message** - Celebratory notification when done
✅ **Pulse Animation** - Breathing circle pulses with the rhythm

### How to Use
```
1. Click the "🫁 Breathe" button
2. Follow the circle as it expands and contracts
3. Read the phase instructions (In/Hold/Out/Calm)
4. Complete the full cycle
5. Click "Done ✓" or click outside
6. Receive a congratulations message!
```

### Benefits
- Reduces stress and anxiety
- Lowers blood pressure
- Improves focus
- Promotes calmness
- Takes only 1 minute per cycle
